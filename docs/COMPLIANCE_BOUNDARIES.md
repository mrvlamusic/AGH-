# Compliance Boundaries

CubPay MVP is a simulation and workflow platform. It does not move, custody, or transmit real customer money.

Real provider adapters must remain disabled until the relevant regulated partners, contracts, legal approvals, licensing, and compliance controls are in place.

## Production controls that must exist before real-money activation
- KYB and UBO verification
- sanctions and restricted-party screening
- purpose-of-payment evidence
- source-of-funds review
- role-based approvals
- immutable audit history
- provider settlement confirmations
- double-entry ledger controls
- daily reconciliation
- incident and dispute procedures

No production integration may be used to conceal transaction origin, destination, counterparties, beneficial ownership, or regulatory exposure.
